Privacy Policy

Ndamathia Poster

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Overview

Ndamathia Poster ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Data We Collect

When you use the Service, we process the following data:

  • TikTok access token: Obtained via TikTok's OAuth Login Kit, used to post content to your TikTok account on your behalf.
  • Video files: Videos you upload are temporarily stored to transmit to TikTok's servers. They are deleted after upload completes.
  • Creator info: We query your TikTok account's available privacy levels and interaction settings to display the correct options in our UI.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to:

  • Authenticate you via TikTok Login Kit
  • Query your available privacy and interaction settings
  • Upload and post your videos to TikTok
  • Check the status of your posts

4. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or process:

  • Your TikTok password (authentication is handled by TikTok OAuth)
  • Personal information beyond what is necessary for posting
  • Analytics or tracking data
  • Third party advertising data

5. Data Storage and Retention

Video files you upload are stored temporarily on our server during the upload process and deleted immediately after the upload to TikTok completes. Access tokens are stored in your browser session and expire according to TikTok's token lifecycle.

6. Third Party Services

The Service integrates with TikTok via TikTok's Content Posting API and Login Kit. TikTok's own Privacy Policy governs your use of TikTok. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke our access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok settings
  • Request deletion of any stored data
  • Withdraw consent for the Service at any time by discontinuing use

8. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. Access tokens are transmitted over HTTPS. Video files are stored only during the upload window.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: ndamathia.poster@gmail.com

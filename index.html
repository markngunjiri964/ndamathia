Features

Everything in one post, nothing you didn’t choose

Direct posting

Upload an MP4 and publish straight to your TikTok account. No screen recording, no manual upload step.

Full privacy control

Public, followers, mutuals, or self only — you pick the audience for every post. Nothing is pre-selected for you.

Interaction settings

Comments, duets, and stitch are off by default. You opt each one in per post, exactly the way you want it.

Self-hosted

Runs on your own machine. Video files are deleted the moment TikTok confirms the upload — no third-party harvesting.

Compliance built in

Follows TikTok’s Content Sharing Guidelines: commercial-content disclosure, no defaults on privacy, capabilities queried first.

Token-based auth

Connects through TikTok Login Kit OAuth. Your password never reaches this server. Revoke access any time from your TikTok settings.

How it works

From file to feed in five steps

  1. Connect your account. Authorize through TikTok’s OAuth. We request only the scopes needed: user info, video publish, and video list.
  2. Upload your video. Pick a vertical MP4 (up to 500 MB). It’s stored only for the transfer and deleted after TikTok confirms receipt.
  3. Choose privacy & interactions. Select a privacy level — none is pre-selected, per TikTok’s guidelines — and toggle comments, duets, and stitch.
  4. Publish. We submit the post through the Content Posting API. The video appears on your profile with the settings you chose.
  5. Track status. Each post returns a publish ID you can check. TikTok processes uploads asynchronously.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you store my TikTok password?

No. Authentication is handled entirely by TikTok’s Login Kit OAuth flow. We receive an access token, never your password. You can revoke access any time from your TikTok account settings.

What happens to my video files after upload?

Files live on the server only during the transfer to TikTok’s servers and are deleted once the upload completes. We don’t retain or analyze your content.

Is this an official TikTok product?

No. Ndamathia Poster is an independent tool that integrates with TikTok’s Content Posting API and Login Kit, built to comply with TikTok’s Content Sharing Guidelines for Direct Post UX.

Why is there no default privacy level?

TikTok’s Content Sharing Guidelines require the creator to actively choose a privacy level for each post. Nothing is pre-selected, so you make an informed decision about who can see your content.

Can I schedule posts for later?

Today the tool supports immediate publishing. Scheduled posting is on the roadmap and will use TikTok’s scheduled-post capabilities once they’re available in the Content Posting API.

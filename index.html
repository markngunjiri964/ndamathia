How it works

  1. Connect your TikTok account. Click “Connect TikTok” and authorize via TikTok’s OAuth. We request only the scopes needed: user info, video publish, and video list.
  2. Upload your video. Select a vertical MP4 file (up to 500MB). We temporarily store it for the upload transfer, then delete it after TikTok confirms receipt.
  3. Choose privacy and interaction settings. Select your privacy level (no default is pre-selected per TikTok guidelines). Toggle comments, duets, and stitch off or on. Disclose commercial content if applicable.
  4. Publish. We submit the post through TikTok’s Content Posting API. The video appears on your TikTok profile with the settings you chose.
  5. Check status. Track the publish status of each post. TikTok processes uploads asynchronously.

FAQ

Do you store my TikTok password?

No. Authentication is handled entirely by TikTok’s Login Kit OAuth flow. We receive an access token, never your password. You can revoke our access at any time from your TikTok account settings.

What happens to my video files after upload?

Video files are stored temporarily on our server only during the upload transfer to TikTok’s servers. They are deleted immediately after the upload completes. We do not retain or analyze your content.

Is this an official TikTok product?

No. Ndamathia Poster is an independent tool that integrates with TikTok’s Content Posting API and Login Kit. It is built to comply with TikTok’s Content Sharing Guidelines for Direct Post UX.

Why is there no default privacy level?

Per TikTok’s Content Sharing Guidelines, the creator must actively choose a privacy level for each post. No default value is pre-selected. This ensures you make an informed decision about who can see your content.

Can I schedule posts for later?

The current version supports immediate publishing. Scheduled posting is on the roadmap and will use TikTok’s scheduled post capabilities once available in the Content Posting API.

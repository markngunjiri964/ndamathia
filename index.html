About

Ndamathia Poster is a web-based content publishing interface built for creators who manage their own content pipeline. Users connect their TikTok account via OAuth, upload original vertical videos, set captions, choose privacy levels, and control interaction settings before posting. The tool queries each creator's available capabilities and interaction permissions, and discloses commercial or AI-generated content where applicable.
